# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in LearnSlop a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age (within our audience), body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

LearnSlop is a community for people learning to program. Many of us are beginners, so we aim to act in ways that keep the project welcoming, open and healthy. The project is built for learners aged 13 and over, and is not aimed at children under 13.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a good community includes:

* Being kind and patient with people who are new to programming
* Answering questions without making anyone feel foolish for asking them
* Giving feedback on code and lessons that is specific, constructive and respectful
* Accepting feedback gracefully and learning from mistakes
* Focusing on what is best for learners and for the community as a whole

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

* Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Mocking someone's skill level or the way they write code
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as an email or home address, without their explicit permission
* Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a learning community

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, lessons and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through a private channel. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report privately on GitHub. Please do not report conduct concerns in public issues or pull requests.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers will follow these steps in deciding the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, and a permanent ban.
